Before you apply

A clear checklist for your driver enquiry.

Taking a few minutes to prepare can make your first conversation with Arain Motors more useful. Use the checklist below to get your details ready and make your enquiry easier.

01

Know the opportunity you want

Think about whether you want to drive using your own vehicle, ask about vehicle availability, or simply learn about the process. Describe your goal in the message field.

02

Prepare basic driving information

Be ready to explain your licence status, relevant experience and the kind of vehicle you can use. Requirements may differ by city, role and third-party platform policy.

03

Use accurate contact details

Double-check spelling of your name and email, include a reachable phone number, and select a contact method you regularly use.

04

Ask about terms before agreeing

Before accepting an arrangement, request clear information about any charges, commissions, deposits, vehicle terms, responsibilities and cancellation conditions. Get important terms in writing.

05

Keep personal information safe

Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or banking credentials. Only provide documents through a verified and appropriate process if they are genuinely required later.

06

Wait for confirmation

A submitted form is a request to be contacted, not a registration confirmation. Do not assume a job, vehicle, income or platform account is guaranteed until terms are independently confirmed.

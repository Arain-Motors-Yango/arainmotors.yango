HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from interest to clarity.

You don't need to figure everything out before reaching out. Start with the basics, then use the conversation to understand the requirements relevant to you.

STEP 01
↗

Send your interest

Complete the rider enquiry form with your name, contact details, city and basic information about your driving and vehicle situation.

STEP 02
⌕

Review the requirements

Discuss your location, licence status, experience and vehicle availability so the next conversation can focus on what matters to you.

STEP 03
◎

Get guidance

Ask questions about the process and clarify which requirements, checks or further steps may apply to your circumstances.

STEP 04
✓

Decide what comes next

Once the relevant details are confirmed, you can decide whether to proceed and follow any verified onboarding instructions provided.

i
Good to know: Sending an enquiry is an initial step, not a guarantee of acceptance or platform activation. Requirements, availability and any onboarding decision depend on the relevant checks and confirmation.
BEFORE YOU START

A little preparation goes a long way.

Having a few basics ready can make your enquiry clearer and help the team understand what guidance you need.

✓

Your contact details

Use a phone number or email address where you can be reached about your question.

✓

Your city or area

Share where you plan to drive, because availability and requirements can vary by location.

✓

Licence and experience status

Be ready to explain whether you hold a valid driving licence and roughly how much driving experience you have.

✓

Your vehicle situation and questions

Let us know whether you have a vehicle or need to understand possible options. Don't send identity-document numbers or upload sensitive documents through this enquiry.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Clear information, sensible next steps.

The goal is to help you understand the process before you make decisions or share additional information.

What you can do

  • Ask questions about rider opportunities and the general process.
  • Explain your location, licence status and vehicle availability.
  • Review any requirements shared with you before deciding how to proceed.
  • Use the website contact form if your question is not covered here.

What to confirm first

  • Which eligibility and vehicle requirements apply to your situation.
  • Whether an opportunity is currently available in your area.
  • Any official platform onboarding, verification or approval steps.
  • Any costs or commitments before agreeing to them.
QUICK QUESTIONS

Still wondering about something?

Here are a few common points to keep in mind as you get started.

Do I need to have every detail ready?

No. Start with the basic enquiry and ask for guidance about anything you're unsure of. Share only information relevant to your question.

Does submitting the form guarantee approval?

No. The enquiry helps start a conversation. Eligibility, current availability and any platform decision must be confirmed separately.

What if I don't have a vehicle yet?

Choose the option that best describes your situation in the form and ask the team what requirements or options can be explained.

Where can I get more details?

Read the Driver guide, explore the FAQs, or use the Contact form.

Ready to take the first step?

Send a rider enquiry and let us know what you need help understanding. You can review the details before deciding what to do next.

QUICK ANSWERS

Questions before you start?

Here are a few useful starting points. You can send an enquiry even if you still have questions.

Browse all FAQs ↗
Can I enquire if I'm not sure which option suits me?
Yes. Choose the option closest to your interest in the form and use the message field to explain what you'd like help understanding.
What details are helpful to prepare?
Your name, reachable contact details, city, general vehicle situation and any questions you want to ask are useful starting points.
Can I ask about motorbike rider options?
Yes. Tell us you're interested in a motorbike-based option so the team can guide you on what to check for your location.
What if I need more guidance first?
Read the driver guide or preparation checklist, then send your questions through the rider enquiry form when you're ready.

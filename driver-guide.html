Quick answers

Driver guide FAQs.

Useful basics before you reach out to the team.

Do I need to know every requirement before enquiring?

No. Start with your basic details and the type of opportunity you want to explore. The team can help explain which current requirements apply to your situation.

What if I do not have a vehicle yet?

You can still explain your situation in the enquiry and ask whether any relevant vehicle options are currently available. Ask about applicable terms and costs before deciding.

What information should I keep ready?

Prepare your reachable contact details, preferred city, general driving or riding experience, vehicle availability and any questions you want answered.

Should I send sensitive information in my first message?

Share only basic details needed to discuss your enquiry. Never send passwords, one-time codes or banking credentials. Confirm a suitable secure process before sharing sensitive documents.

How do I ask about current eligibility?

Use the rider enquiry form and clearly describe your location, experience and vehicle situation. Ask the Arain Motors team to confirm the current requirements relevant to you.

Where can I read about the next steps?

Visit the How it works guide → or review Before you apply → for additional preparation tips.
